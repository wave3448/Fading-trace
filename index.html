STATEMENT / 001
A PLACE FOR ART TO BE SEEN

Not content.
An encounter.

We believe visibility is not the same as attention. Reach is not the same as connection. A number is not a witness.

Fading Trace is for work that does not want to perform forever—for the artist who wants to be encountered, not optimized. Here, the work is placed into a moment. The artist controls the atmosphere, the threshold, and the disappearance.

Your work does not have to perform forever to matter.

THE INTERVAL / 001NOT A FEED
THE FADING TRACE PROTOCOL

A work with
an opening.
A work with a closing.

Most online galleries preserve the work indefinitely. Most platforms ask it to perform forever. Fading Trace gives the artist a different instrument: one defined window of attention, one deliberate encounter, one chosen ending.

01 / OPENSet the threshold.

The artist chooses the work, the context, the start, and the length of the interval.

02 / WITNESSHold the moment.

Visitors enter a live room rather than a feed. Presence changes the work and attention becomes part of the piece.

03 / CLOSELeave an afterimage.

At the end, the full work leaves the live web. A minimal record remains only if the artist chooses.

THE DIFFERENCE: this is not permanent storage, virtual real estate, or algorithmic reach. It is a public ritual for work that deserves a beginning and an end.
